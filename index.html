FIFO Queue Architecture & Goal

Tactical Squad Management & Computer Science Fundamentals

THE GOAL OF THE APP

The main goal of this application is to visually demonstrate First-In, First-Out (FIFO) Queue Data Structure operations through an interactive, real-time 11v11 African football match. It bridges theoretical computer science data structures with real-world sports subbing logic.

WHY THE QUEUE IS NECESSARY

Queues guarantee order and fairness. During live, high-pressure match operations, multiple tactical changes take place. A FIFO Queue prevents chaotic subbing, eliminates race conditions, and ensures substitutes enter the pitch strictly in the precise chronological order they were instructed to warm up.

WHAT THE QUEUES ARE FOR

1. Index 0 (Front / Head): Holds the active substitute authorized to enter the match next.
2. Enqueue (Rear / Tail): Adds players warming up to the back of the queue.
3. Dequeue: Removes the head player when the substitution is triggered onto the pitch.
